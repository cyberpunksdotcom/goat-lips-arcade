# GOAT of the Week: official rules (DRAFT)

> **Draft for review, not legal advice.** Have the owner or GM approve it, and have someone familiar with Florida promotion rules look it over before launch. Items in **[brackets]** need a decision (see `DECISIONS.md`).

**1. Who runs it.** Goat Lips Chew & Brewhouse, Pensacola, Florida ("Goat Lips").

**2. Free to play.** No purchase is necessary to play or win, and buying something doesn't improve your chances. The winner is decided by skill: the highest verified score.

**3. Contest weeks.** Each week starts Monday at 12:00 AM Central Time and ends just before the next Monday at 12:00 AM Central Time. Scores count toward the week in which our server receives them.

**4. How to play.** Play Slide 'Em at play.goatlips.com and enter three initials to submit your score. Only finished games played on the official site count. Scores are checked by our system before they appear on the leaderboard.

**5. Winning.**
- The highest verified score for the week wins.
- Ties go to the score submitted first.
- **[One prize per person per calendar month. If the top player already won that month, the prize goes to the next-highest eligible score.]**

**6. Prize.**
- **[Prize description, e.g., one free (non-alcoholic) item up to $X in value]**.
- No cash value. Not transferable. Can't be combined with other offers.

**7. Claiming.**
- The winning device shows a private claim code.
- Show it to a manager or bartender at Goat Lips within **[14]** days after the week ends. Unclaimed prizes are forfeited.
- Staff may ask you to replay or confirm details.

**8. Eligibility.** Open to everyone except Goat Lips employees and their immediate households **[confirm]**. Players under 18 should have a parent's or guardian's permission **[confirm]**.

**9. Fair play.** Goat Lips may remove scores or disqualify players that appear to involve cheating, automation, multiple identities, offensive initials, or tampering with the game. Goat Lips' decisions are final.

**10. Privacy.** We store your initials, your score and a record of your game moves to verify scores. We don't collect your name, email or phone number. See the privacy note at play.goatlips.com/privacy.

**11. Changes.** Goat Lips may change or end the contest at any time by posting updated rules on the site. Changes take effect at the start of the next contest week.
